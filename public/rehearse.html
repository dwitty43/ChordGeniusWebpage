How to Rehearse with Chord Genius
Tap to expand the rehearsal & autoscroll guide

Welcome to the Chord Genius Rehearsal Studio. Here you can lock in your timing with our high-fidelity metronome, analyze chord groupings to find the original key of any song, calculate transposing capo frets, and practice with a smooth hands-free scrolling sheet display.

  • 1. Loading a Song: Go to the Convert tab, search or upload a song, and click the ⚡ Take to Rehearsal button. This grabs your active song text, target key, and tempo settings, and transfers them here instantly!
  • 2. Metronome Rehearsal: Use the metronome control pane to set your target BPM, select a time signature (2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8), and click Start (or press the Space bar) to play the audio click.
  • 3. Metronome-Synced Autoscroll: Switch on the Sync Autoscroll with Metronome toggle. When active, starting the metronome will automatically scroll your chords sheet continuously at a velocity directly proportional to the active BPM! Stopping the metronome or toggling off the sync will immediately pause the scroll.
  • 4. Fine-Tuning Scroll Velocity: Adjust the Scroll Speed slider (0.2x to 3.0x speed factor) to speed up or slow down the scrolling rate to perfectly match the length and layout of your chart.
  • 5. Chord Fingering Tooltips: Stuck on a complex shape? Simply hover your cursor over any chord in the rehearsal sheet to view visual, theme-aware guitar fingerings in real-time!
  • 6. Free-play Mode: Feel free to drag or scroll the sheet manually at any time. Chord Genius will dynamically adapt and continue smooth autoscrolling from your new position without snapping back.
⚡ Pro-Tip: Connect your laptop or tablet to a stand on stage, set your BPM, toggle Autoscroll Sync, and play hands-free!
