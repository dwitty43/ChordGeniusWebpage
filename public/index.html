How to Use Chord Genius
Tap to expand the guide

Chord Genius is designed for professional transposition, capo calculation, and setlist binding.

  • 1. Search: Enter a song query to instantly scrape and transpose charts from the web.
  • 2. Upload: Drag & drop standard .txt, .docx, .pdf, or ChordPro (.pro) files.
  • 3. Custom Tuning: Transpose dynamically to any key, or select "Nashville Numbers". Use the Capo selector to let the optimizer do the fingering math for you.
  • 4. Interactive Stage: Once converted, review chord diagrams by hovering over the highlighted chords in the Stage View.
  • 5. Live Editing: Tap "Editor" to real-time edit the chart text, automatically syncing with your preview.
  • 6. Setlist Packet: Build a custom show queue and download them compiled as a single unified PDF/Word packet!
