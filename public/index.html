What is Chord Genius?

Whether you need to shift a song's key to fit a singer's vocal range, or prep a chart for a full band, Chord Genius handles the musical math for you — instantly converting chords to any key or the Nashville Number System.

How to Use It

  • 1. Find Your Chart: Search by song name to pull from Ultimate Guitar, or upload your own .txt, .docx, or .pdf file.
  • 2. Verify the Key: We auto-detect the key — override it with Orig Key if needed.
  • 3. Transpose or Convert: Pick a To Key to transpose, or leave it on "Nashville" to generate number charts. Already in numbers? Select "Nashville Numbers" as your Orig Key.
  • 4. Download: Choose .DOCX for easy editing or .PDF for a stage-ready print, then hit convert.
♫ Heads up: Web-scraped charts depend on the original author's accuracy. Always verify the starting key sounds right!
