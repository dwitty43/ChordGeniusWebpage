🎸 Chord Genius Chart Generator

Convert chord charts between keys or to universal Nashville Numbers.

How to Use

  • Search: Type a song. We'll scrape it from Ultimate Guitar.
  • Upload: Have a .txt or .docx? Upload it directly.
  • Transposition: Leave "To Key" blank to convert the chart into Nashville Numbers. Want to transpose a song? Enter your desired key (e.g., 'G'). You can even use this to turn an existing Nashville chart back into standard chords!
⚠️ Note: Auto-scraped keys can occasionally be wrong. If the output looks incorrect, verify the original key and enter it manually in the "Orig Key" box!
