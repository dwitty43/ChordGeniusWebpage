How to Use Chord Genius
Tap to expand the guide

Whether you need to shift a song's key to fit a singer's range, or prep a chart for the full band — Chord Genius handles the musical math instantly.

  • 1. Find Your Chart: Search by song name to pull from Ultimate Guitar, or upload your own .txt, .docx, or .pdf.
  • 2. Verify the Key: We auto-detect the original key. Override it with Orig Key if needed.
  • 3. Transpose & Capo Optimizer: Pick a target To Key to transpose, or select "Nashville" to generate a number chart. Let our smart Capo Optimizer automatically calculate the optimal capo fret setting to minimize complex barre chords while keeping the target sounding key.
  • 4. Interactive Preview & Edit: Hit Preview or Convert, then swiftly switch between the live text Editor tab to adjust lyric/chord alignment and the Interactive Preview tab to rehearse.
  • 5. Visual Chord Diagrams: Stuck on a voicing? Simply hover your cursor over any chord in the interactive sheet to view organic, theme-aware guitar fingering guides.
  • 6. Metronome & Autoscroll: Open the Rehearse pane to activate a high-fidelity metronome sound click and activate smooth autoscrolling synced to your song's BPM.
♫ Charts scraped from the web depend on the original author's accuracy — always verify the starting key!
